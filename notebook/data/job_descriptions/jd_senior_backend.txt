Job Title: Senior Backend Engineer (Python / Cloud)
Company: NimbusPay Technologies
Location: Bengaluru, India (Hybrid)
Department: Platform Engineering

About the Role:
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to design and scale the payment
processing services that power millions of daily transactions. You will own
critical microservices end-to-end, mentor mid-level engineers, and drive
engineering best practices across the team.

Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and maintain high-throughput backend microservices in Python.
- Architect cloud-native systems on GCP (Cloud Run, Pub/Sub, BigQuery).
- Build and optimize RESTful and gRPC APIs.
- Design relational and NoSQL data models (PostgreSQL, Redis).
- Implement CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code (Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform).
- Ensure observability, reliability, and security of production systems.
- Mentor junior engineers and lead code reviews.

Required Skills:
- Strong proficiency in Python.
- Experience with at least one cloud platform (GCP preferred, AWS/Azure acceptable).
- Solid understanding of microservices architecture and distributed systems.
- Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes.
- Strong SQL skills and experience with PostgreSQL.
- Experience designing and consuming REST APIs.
- Familiarity with message queues (Pub/Sub, Kafka, or RabbitMQ).

Nice to Have:
- Experience with Terraform or other IaC tools.
- Exposure to the fintech / payments domain.
- Experience with gRPC and Protocol Buffers.
- Knowledge of Go or Java.

Qualifications:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or a related field.
- 5+ years of professional backend software engineering experience.

Employment Type: Full-time
Experience Level: Senior (5-8 years)
